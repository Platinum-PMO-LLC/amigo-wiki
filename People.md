# 👥 People Management

> People and resource management capabilities within the AMIGO platform for managing users, roles, and stakeholder engagement.

---

## 📖 Overview

The People module in AMIGO provides comprehensive functionality for managing all human resources involved in your digital transformation program. From defining job roles to managing stakeholder groups, AMIGO ensures proper resource allocation and communication channels.

---

---

## 💼 Business Usage

### PMO Application

People management in AMIGO supports the critical resource planning and stakeholder engagement activities essential for program success. By providing comprehensive tracking of roles, resources, and communication channels, program teams can ensure proper staffing and stakeholder alignment throughout the transformation.

### Common Use Cases

- **Resource Planning**: Define workstreams and assign specialized resources to projects
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Identify and communicate with key stakeholder groups
- **Security Administration**: Define job roles with appropriate security profiles
- **Vendor Management**: Track solution integrator involvement across programs
