# 🛡️ Governance

> RAID management and governance capabilities within the AMIGO platform for managing Risks, Actions, Issues, and Decisions.

---

## 📖 Overview

The Governance module in AMIGO provides comprehensive functionality for managing all governance activities throughout your digital transformation program. From risk management to lessons learned, AMIGO ensures proper controls and documentation through the integrated core.

## 💼 Business Usage

### PMO Application

Governance in AMIGO supports the critical oversight and control activities essential for program success. By providing comprehensive RAID management and integrated communication tools, program teams can proactively manage risks, resolve issues, and make timely decisions.

### Common Use Cases

- **Risk Management**: Identify, assess, and mitigate risks with RACI-based ownership
- **Issue Resolution**: Track issues through resolution with impact analysis on deliverables
- **Decision Tracking**: Monitor decision velocity to prevent program delays
- **Lessons Learned**: Capture and share knowledge for continuous improvement
- **Change Control**: Govern scope changes through formal approval workflows
