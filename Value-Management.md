# 📈 Value Management

> Value and financial management capabilities within the AMIGO platform for tracking goals, benefits, and costs.

---

## 📖 Overview

The Value Management module in AMIGO provides comprehensive functionality for defining goals, tracking benefits, and managing costs throughout your digital transformation program. From strategic objectives to detailed cost tracking, AMIGO ensures alignment between program activities and business value.

---

## 💼 Business Usage

### PMO Application

Value management in AMIGO supports the critical business case and benefits realization activities essential for demonstrating program ROI. By linking strategic goals to measurable objectives and tracking both benefits and costs, program teams can demonstrate value delivery to stakeholders.

### Common Use Cases

- **Business Case Development**: Define goals, objectives, and expected benefits
- **Benefits Realization**: Track actual benefit delivery against planned targets
- **Cost Control**: Monitor program costs at summary and detail levels
- **ROI Reporting**: Generate financial reports for executive stakeholders
