## 💼 Business Usage

### PMO Application

Projects are the primary execution unit within AMIGO, representing temporary endeavors to deliver specific outcomes. They contain deliverables, work packages, and all governance artifacts required for successful delivery.

### Common Use Cases

| Use Case | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **System Implementation** | Deploy new software or platforms |
| **Process Improvement** | Optimize business operations |
| **Product Development** | Create new products or services |
| **Infrastructure** | Build or upgrade technical infrastructure |
| **Compliance** | Meet regulatory requirements |

### Project Artifacts

- **Project Charter**: Formal authorization and scope
- **Deliverables**: Major outputs and milestones
- **Work Packages**: Detailed work breakdown
- **RACI Chart**: Role assignments
- **Risk/Issue Logs**: Governance items

### Project Lifecycle

1. **Initiation** - Charter approval and kickoff
2. **Planning** - Scope, schedule, and resource planning
3. **Execution** - Work package delivery
4. **Monitoring** - Progress tracking and governance
5. **Closure** - Handover and lessons learned

the historical comments, which is that ledger of all the different changes we're making to the project record of the project information from a business perspective. And every time we go and make a change, we're going to update that historical comment and provide that business perspective of why we made that change. Because it's always good to document this type of information because you never know what comments that you make today may be important months or years from now.
