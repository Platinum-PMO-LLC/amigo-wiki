## Available features

### Add Objective
<p align="justify">Objectives are tied to our goals, which are tied to our program. And we have indirect relationships with Portfolios and Organizations. Still, mostly we tie our Objectives to our Goals, and we can drive that from our Program functions within the AMIGO platform.
By using the `Add Objective` button, Objectives are tied to Goals. Most importantly, an Objective can be tied to one and only one Goal.</p>
